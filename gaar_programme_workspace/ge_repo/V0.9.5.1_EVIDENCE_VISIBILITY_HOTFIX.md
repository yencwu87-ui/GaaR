# v0.9.5.1 — Evidence Visibility Hotfix

## Problem
In the progressive Review Workspace, after Evidence was completed, the `Your reading` stage showed the reading controls but did not keep the underlying evidence visible/readable. Reviewers had to leave the current task to return to Evidence, increasing cognitive load and making it easy to assess without seeing the source material.

## Fix
Added a read-only `Evidence used for this review` panel to every post-evidence stage:

- Your reading — expanded by default
- AI assessment — collapsed
- Compare — collapsed
- Challenge — collapsed
- Decision — collapsed

The panel surfaces:
- evidence text in a selectable read-only viewer
- attached filename when applicable
- plugin/provider/target metadata
- observation timestamp and truncated hash where present
- plugin locator/excerpt when applicable

Editing remains on the dedicated Evidence step. The hotfix does not change evidence authority, blind-read ordering, comparison, challenge, or decision semantics.
