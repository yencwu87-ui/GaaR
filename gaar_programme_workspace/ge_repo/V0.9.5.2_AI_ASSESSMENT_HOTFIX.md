# v0.9.5.2 — AI Assessment Failure Hotfix

## Problem
The Review workspace could show:

> The AI assessment failed; no AI rating was produced.

The underlying assessor error was too opaque, and the existing **Retry** action in the `is_error()` branch merely reran the page rather than issuing a new assessor call.

## Fixes

1. **Actionable Ollama errors**
   - HTTP failures include status, configured model, URL, and response detail.
   - Connection failures explicitly identify the Ollama service/model problem.
   - Timeouts explicitly identify the 300s timeout and suggest checking model/service responsiveness.
   - Malformed Ollama responses identify the unexpected response shape.

2. **Retry actually retries**
   - The Review workspace now makes a fresh `propose()` call when an assessment is in an error state.
   - A successful retry appends a new `proposed` governance event and advances to the AI assessment step.
   - A failed retry remains an error and preserves the actionable reason.

3. **No governance weakening**
   - A failed assessment still produces no AI rating.
   - No error is converted into `none` or any other rating.
   - Blind-read ordering, challenge gates, evidence authority, and decision admissibility are unchanged.
