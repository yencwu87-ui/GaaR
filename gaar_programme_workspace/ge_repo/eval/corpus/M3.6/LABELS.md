## Boundary for this control

**Defined in `requirements/mas.yaml` under `controls.M3.6`, not here.** That file is what
the assessor is judged against and what a reviewer reads. Restating it here created two
definitions of `full` for one control and they diverged. Reproduced below for reading
convenience only; if the two disagree, the requirement file wins and these labels must be
re-argued.

**Requirement.** AI systems are evaluated and independently validated against defined
criteria before deployment, with results, limitations, residual risk and re-validation
triggers documented.

**Elements (7) — every applicable one must be evidenced for `full`:**

- `e1` — Evaluation measures and acceptable performance thresholds are defined against the AI's objectives before testing begins.
- `e2` — Evaluation and testing results exist for the specific AI use case, system or model on representative data and under plausible conditions of use.
- `e3` — Testing covers relevant performance and failure conditions, including edge cases and, where appropriate, robustness, sub-population, fairness and adversarial testing.
- `e4` — Pre-deployment review is performed by parties not involved in development or deployment, with scope and independence proportionate to assessed risk materiality.
- `e5` — For high-risk-materiality AI, formal independent validation is performed by competent and objective personnel or functions and provides effective challenge to developers.
- `e6` — Validation covers conceptual soundness, data-input suitability and quality, implementation integrity, evaluation and testing results, explainability and fairness, and assumptions, limitations and mitigants.
- `e7` — Validation and review outcomes are recorded with findings, limitations, conditions for use, residual risk and an accountable owner before deployment, and re-validation triggers are defined and proportionate to assessed risk materiality.

| Rating | Condition (from the requirement file) |
|---|---|
| full | All applicable requirement elements are evidenced with current, attributable and sufficient design or operating evidence; no material mandatory element remains unsubstantiated. |
| partial | Some applicable requirement elements are evidenced, but one or more required elements or operating evidence is missing or incomplete. |
| none | The control requirement is not evidenced beyond generic intent, policy aspiration or unrelated material. |

Maturity is judged separately and is not the label under test here.
