# AI Governance Engine v0.6 — Evidence-Lineage Governance

## Purpose

v0.6 makes reviewer-claim grounding a hard governance boundary. The Challenger can no longer
create a substantive rebuttal against an unregistered reviewer proposition.

## Increment delivered

1. **Claim Register** — every reviewer proposition receives a stable `claim_id` and deterministic
   fingerprint.
2. **Grounding metadata** — vetted claims carry grounding status and evidence identifiers.
3. **Evidence lineage** — factual pointers receive deterministic `evidence_id` values.
4. **Challenge admissibility gate** — reviewer-read challenges must reference a vetted `claim_id`.
5. **Strong-rebuttal gate** — only claims whose supplied evidence was classified `contradicted` can
   remain `strong`. Supported/unsupported/unclear claims can generate only non-substantive refinement
   or evidence requests.
6. **Fail-closed claim vetting** — if reviewer claim vetting fails, the reviewer-read Challenger is
   blocked rather than silently reverting to generic evidence/requirement attack.
7. **Dossier provenance** — challenge dossiers preserve `claim_id` and `evidence_id`.
8. **Backward compatibility** — the disagreement pass retains its own element-scope gate and does
   not incorrectly require the reviewer Claim Register.

## Governance invariant

> No reviewer-read challenge is substantive unless it targets a vetted claim and the supplied
> evidence has been classified as materially contradictory to that claim.
