# WB-120 — Evidence Scout Agent

Status: **LAB-GREEN / CONNECTOR EXPANSION PENDING**

Adds a bounded evidence-discovery agent over explicitly approved evidence roots. It ranks and deduplicates evidence candidates, reports uncovered requirement elements, and records append-only Scout runs.

The Scout cannot bind evidence, decide sufficiency, assign maturity, or issue a governance decision. Human or Conductor policy must decide whether proposed candidates enter a review bundle.

Validation: 5/5 dedicated tests green.
