# Pilot Evaluation — Contact Centre Assistant (CCA)

Prepared by: Digital Channels Delivery, with input from Contact Centre Operations
Ref: CCA/PILOT-EVAL/2026-07
Status: For decision — expansion to Tranche 2 teams
Period covered: 3 March – 26 June 2026
Circulated: 9 July 2026

---

## 1. What the assistant does

CCA is a retrieval-augmented generative assistant available to contact-centre agents in the
agent desktop. It performs three functions:

- **Draft** — proposes a response to a customer enquiry, which the agent edits and sends
- **Summarise** — condenses a case history into a short brief at handover
- **Suggest** — proposes a next action from the standard action list

Retrieval is over the internal product knowledge base (KB), the policy library, and the
customer's own case history in the CRM. The agent is always the sender; CCA cannot send,
commit the bank, or write to the customer record.

Pilot covered 41 agents across two teams (Cards Servicing, Deposits Servicing). 18,442 assisted
interactions.

---

## 2. Configuration as tested

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Base model | Vendor-hosted, `cl-mid-2` endpoint, version pinned `2026-02-11` |
| Retrieval | Hybrid BM25 + dense, top-8 passages, KB snapshot 2026-02-28 |
| Grounding | Response must cite at least one retrieved passage or return "no source found" |
| Temperature | 0.2 |
| Guardrails | Pre-send classifier for PII echo, financial advice, and commitment language |
| Logging | Full prompt, retrieval set, generation, agent edit-distance, send/discard |

The pinned model version was changed once during the pilot, on 14 May 2026, following a vendor
deprecation notice. Evaluation results in §4 are split before and after that change where the
sample supports it.

---

## 3. What we tested

Test design was run by Delivery with review by Contact Centre Operations. Three streams:

**Stream A — scripted scenarios (n=340).** Constructed enquiries across the 12 highest-volume
intents, with a known correct answer agreed with the relevant product teams.

**Stream B — adversarial and difficult (n=180).** Deliberately hard inputs: enquiries whose answer
is not in the KB, enquiries where two KB articles conflict, enquiries phrased to elicit a
commitment ("can you confirm you'll waive it"), prompt-injection attempts embedded in pasted
customer emails, mixed-language input, and enquiries about products the bank does not offer.

**Stream C — live shadow (n=18,442).** Production traffic with the assistant available, agent
free to use or ignore. Outcome measured by agent edit distance, send rate, and post-interaction
quality sampling (n=600 sampled by the Quality team against the existing QA rubric).

## 4. Results

### 4.1 Scripted (Stream A)

| Intent group | n | Materially correct | Partially correct | Incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Card transactions & disputes | 74 | 68 | 5 | 1 |
| Fees & charges | 61 | 52 | 7 | 2 |
| Deposit account servicing | 58 | 54 | 3 | 1 |
| Standing instructions | 41 | 35 | 4 | 2 |
| Statements & documents | 38 | 36 | 2 | 0 |
| Fraud & lost card | 30 | 29 | 1 | 0 |
| Remaining six intents | 38 | 30 | 6 | 2 |

Post-model-change (14 May) subsample n=96; materially correct rate 88.5% against 86.2% pre-change.
Difference not significant at this sample size.

### 4.2 Adversarial and difficult (Stream B)

| Condition | n | Behaved as intended | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Answer absent from KB | 42 | 36 | 6 produced a plausible answer with no citation; guardrail caught 4 of the 6 |
| Conflicting KB articles | 24 | 14 | Most commonly picked the more recently updated article without flagging the conflict |
| Commitment elicitation | 30 | 30 | Pre-send classifier blocked all 30 |
| Prompt injection in pasted text | 26 | 21 | 5 followed the injected instruction to alter the summary; none produced a sendable customer response |
| Mixed-language (EN/ZH/MS) | 32 | 25 | Degradation concentrated in Malay-language enquiries |
| Products not offered | 26 | 24 | 2 described a competitor product as if it were the bank's |

Findings from this stream were logged and discussed with the implementation team at the 11 June
working session. Items F-02 (unsupported statements where KB has no answer) and F-05 (prompt
injection altering summaries) were accepted by Delivery as requiring change before expansion.
F-03 (conflicting articles) was accepted as a KB hygiene issue owned by Product Content rather
than a system change.

### 4.3 Live shadow (Stream C)

Suggestion used in some form: 71.3%. Sent with no edit: 22.1%. Median edit distance on used
drafts: 34 characters. Discarded without use: 28.7%.

Quality sampling (n=600) against the existing QA rubric: assisted interactions scored 4.31 mean
versus 4.22 for a matched unassisted sample from the same period. Two assisted interactions were
scored as QA failures; both involved fee information that was correct per the KB but the KB
article itself was out of date.

### 4.4 Coverage of the interaction population

Stream C traffic distribution against the full contact-centre population for the period:

| Segment | Share of pilot traffic | Share of all CC traffic |
|---|---|---|
| Cards Servicing | 54% | 31% |
| Deposits Servicing | 46% | 24% |
| Lending Servicing | 0% | 22% |
| Wealth Servicing | 0% | 14% |
| Collections | 0% | 9% |

Language mix in the pilot was 81% English, 12% Mandarin, 5% Malay, 2% other, against a
contact-centre population of 68% / 17% / 11% / 4%.

The evaluation therefore covers two of five servicing lines. Performance for Lending, Wealth and
Collections enquiries is not evidenced by this pilot. The Malay-language degradation noted at
§4.2 is based on 32 scripted cases and a small live sample and should be treated as indicative.

---

## 5. Known limitations

1. No evidence for three of five servicing lines.
2. Malay-language performance under-sampled relative to the live population and weaker where
   measured.
3. Conflicting-KB behaviour is unresolved and depends on content owned outside this project.
4. Prompt-injection handling is partial; summaries remain affected.
5. The KB snapshot is static. Live retrieval will use the current KB; the gap between the tested
   snapshot and live content is not quantified.
6. Quality sampling used the existing QA rubric, which was not designed to assess AI-assisted
   interactions and has no item covering unsupported statements.

---

## 6. Human review and escalation

The agent operating procedure (CCA-SOP-01, issued 28 February 2026) requires the agent to read
every draft before sending, to verify any figure, date or fee against the source system, and to
discard rather than edit where the draft contains an unsupported claim. Escalation to a team lead
is required where the assistant returns "no source found" on a complaint-coded enquiry.

Adherence was not separately measured during the pilot. The 22.1% send-with-no-edit figure at
§4.3 was raised at the 11 June session as a possible indicator of over-reliance; no conclusion was
reached and Operations has proposed adding an adherence check to the standard QA sampling.

---

## 7. Position for the expansion decision

Delivery's view is that Tranche 2 expansion to Lending Servicing should not proceed on this
evidence, as no Lending enquiries were evaluated. Expansion within Cards and Deposits is
supportable subject to F-02 and F-05 being closed.

Outstanding items F-02, F-03 and F-05 are recorded in the delivery issue log. F-03 sits with
Product Content and has no agreed date.

This pack has not been reviewed by Technology Risk or by the second line. It was prepared by the
delivery team with operational input. A second-line review has been requested for the Tranche 2
decision but had not commenced at the date of circulation.
